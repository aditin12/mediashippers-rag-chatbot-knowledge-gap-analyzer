Marketplace FAQ & Common Questions

Document ID: MS-FAQ-010

Last Updated: October 2023

Q: Can sellers see buyer identities before shortlisting?
No. Buyer identities remain completely hidden until a buyer explicitly shortlists a submission.
Q: Can buyers negotiate payment terms later?
Yes. Payment structures and financial terms may be freely negotiated in the chat module after a title has been shortlisted.
Q: Can sellers submit titles not uploaded on Mediashippers?
No. Only titles that have already been uploaded, processed, and are available on the Mediashippers platform may be submitted to an active deal.
Q: What happens when a deal expires?
Expired deals immediately stop accepting new submissions and disappear from seller discovery results. However, chats for previously shortlisted titles remain active.
Q: Can buyers request exclusive rights only for specific territories?
Yes. Buyers may configure territory-specific exclusivity (e.g., Exclusive in Europe, Non-Exclusive in Asia).
Q: Are dubbed versions supported?
Yes. Buyers may request existing dubbed content, or they may request "dubbing rights" to create their own localized audio.
Q: What payment models are supported?
Supported payment models include:
  • Flat Fee
  • Revenue Share
  • Minimum Guarantee (MG) + Revenue Share
Q: Can platform administrators access negotiations?
Administrators only access chats in the event of formal disputes, fraud investigations, or policy violations. Otherwise, chats are confidential.
